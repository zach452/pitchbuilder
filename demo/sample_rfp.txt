REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Northwind Outdoor Gear — Performance Marketing Agency Search

Company Overview:
Northwind Outdoor Gear is a DTC outdoor apparel and equipment brand doing roughly $8M in annual
revenue, sold primarily through our Shopify store with a growing wholesale channel. We are
seeking a performance marketing agency partner to manage paid social and paid search, with the
goal of profitably scaling customer acquisition while protecting margin.

Business Objectives:
- Grow online revenue by 35% year over year
- Improve blended marketing efficiency ratio (MER) from current ~2.8x to 4x+
- Diversify acquisition channels beyond our current heavy reliance on Meta
- Build a defensible, scalable measurement framework ahead of iOS/privacy changes

Required Capabilities:
- Paid social management (Meta, TikTok)
- Paid search management (Google)
- Creative strategy and testing cadence
- Reporting and measurement, including incrementality testing roadmap

Stated KPIs:
- Blended ROAS target of 4.0x
- CPA under $45 for core acquisition campaigns
- Monthly revenue growth of at least 2.5% MoM

Budget Parameters:
Estimated monthly media budget: $120,000-$150,000, with management fee proposals requested
separately.

Timing:
We intend to select a partner within 6 weeks of RFP distribution, with onboarding to begin
no later than 30 days after award.

Channels Requested:
Meta, Google, TikTok

Deliverables:
- 90-day media plan
- Creative testing framework
- Measurement and reporting cadence proposal
- Case studies from comparable DTC apparel/outdoor brands

Decision Criteria:
- Demonstrated experience with comparable brands and budget sizes
- Clarity and credibility of measurement approach
- Team chemistry and communication style
- Cost competitiveness of management fees

Submission Instructions:
Please submit proposals including a case study, proposed team structure, and pricing by the
deadline communicated separately. Finalists will be invited to a chemistry meeting and final
pitch presentation.
